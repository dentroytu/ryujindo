道From being ripped off to your name on a shutter

This isn't a game about restocking shelves. It's a game about knowing what the thing in front of you is worth — and deciding who you sell it to.

01

The problem: you don't know what you have

Morning in the six-tatami room your grandmother lends you, boxes still taped shut and rent due on Friday. One of those boxes holds a card worth a great deal more than anyone is about to pay you for it.

Kenji offers ¥12,000, cash, no paperwork. He doesn't lie, he doesn't push and he doesn't apologise: he simply knows something you don't. You take it, because Friday is Friday.

02

The fix: appraise before you sell

A card's price isn't a number: condition, foil, set index and Sunday's tournament all move it — the winning element gains 35 %. An appraisal costs money; getting it wrong costs more.

Then there's the other half: every card is worth a different number depending on who buys it. The wholesaler on the corner pays 55 % and pays today. The app pays more and takes its cut, plus postage and a sleeve. A collector in the street overpays for his own set, and the request board pays a premium for the exact card somebody has spent weeks hunting.

03

The move: the shutter on the corner

The 貸店舗 is in plain sight from day one: an empty unit, shutter down, "to let" sign in the window. Four acts — the room, the rent, the conventions and the lease — stand between one and the other.

And you're not alone in the queue: a neighbour is saving for that same unit at his own pace. If he signs first, the price goes up 15 % for good.

品What's in it

Six systems that touch each other, and not one of them is a menu with a button.

札540 cards, and all of them mean something

Six sets of ninety, each card with its element, its health, its attack and the weakness that takes it down. They come from a deterministic catalogue: 090/090 of the 龍神 set is the same card in your game as in anyone else's.

相場A market that moves on its own

Sunday's tournament lifts a whole element by 35 %, and that reshuffles half your collection while you sleep. Buying cheap on Saturday what rises on Sunday is a play — if you know who's going to win.

交換Trading, face to face

At Saturday's market you trade with people who value by rarity and ignore the meta. That's where the margin is — and the person across the table has an eye too, and remembers you next time.

勝負An actual card game

一本勝負: twenty-card decks, three copies maximum, and never more copies than you really own. Played on your shop's tables and at the Sunday tournament.

街A neighbourhood you walk

Nakano, Ameyoko and Akihabara, the covered arcade, the crossing, the station and its platform, a park with cherry trees, and a residential street where nothing is for sale. You walk from one end to the other.

四幕A campaign with an ending

Four acts, from the room full of moving boxes to signing the lease, with a thread of messages tying them together. There is an ending, and you can reach it.
